NFC Tags

Ordinary tags only · private Autoprod tool

What this app can and cannot do

It can

  • Read ordinary NFC stickers and tags that store a link, some text, a contact card or Wi-Fi details (these are called NDEF tags).
  • Write those same kinds of records onto a blank, writable sticker or tag.
  • Keep a library of tags on this phone, and export or import that list.

It cannot

  • Copy, clone or emulate a card or fob. Your phone cannot stand in for a keycard.
  • Read most office or hotel keycards. Those are usually encrypted, or use a type Chrome cannot open.
  • Read most car keys or many door fobs. Those often use a different radio (125 kHz) that phones do not support.
  • Work on iPhone. Apple does not let websites use NFC this way.

Hold the card, fob or sticker against the back of your phone, near the top. Keep it still for a couple of seconds.

Write a blank tag

This overwrites a writable NFC sticker or tag. It will not work on a locked keycard, a car key or most door fobs.

If you later save this to your library, the password is stored on this device only.

After you tap Write, hold a blank writable tag against the back of your phone, near the top, until Chrome finishes.
